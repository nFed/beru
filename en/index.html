How we work.

Four clear stages. At each one you know exactly what is happening and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Intro call

    We learn your needs, your audience and the goals of the project. No cost, no commitment.

  2. 02

    Concept and planning

    We present a creative concept, a budget and a detailed schedule for your approval.

  3. 03

    Shoot and production

    A professional crew, advanced equipment and precise production management. On the day, it all comes together.

  4. 04

    Post and delivery

    Editing, sound and color. Delivered in every format you need, with full support.

About Leeu Productions.

Leeu Productions was founded in 2010 on one belief: a good film can change the way people think, feel and decide.

Started by director and producer Shmuel Beru. Since then we have produced hundreds of projects for Israel’s biggest brands, international companies and leading media.

Our production teams of directors, producers, cinematographers and editors are put together around what each project needs.
